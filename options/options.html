Site Request Settings ·

The email address of the company that approves your site requests. Nothing is sent until you add it.

If they only look at a request after you call, add their number here. After each request, you see "Call this number so they review your request."

Account details (optional)

Filled-in details are added at the bottom of every request, so the request can be matched to your account. Empty ones are left out.

Email

Only if you are signed in to more than one Google account: 0 is the first account, 1 the second, and so on. You can also type the Gmail address itself.

You can use {url} for the full address, {domain} for the site name, {target} for what you ask for (the whole site — the domain and known subdomains, one address per line — or just the page), {ask} for the matching request sentence, {page} for the page you were on (whole-site requests only), {date} for the date and time, {reason} for the reason shown on the block page, and {why} for the reason you pick and your details. A line with {reason}, {page} or {why} is left out when it has no value.

The email is sent neatly laid out, in the extension's colors, with your account details in their own part. Turn off to send plain text.

Reasons

Pick one with one tap when you send a request. Its text goes in the email as "Reason: …".

    Used by the right-click menu and picked first in the popup. To choose another one from the right-click menu, use "Request with reason…".

    Every 30 minutes, the sites you requested in the last 30 days are opened in the background. When one opens, you get a notification and it is marked Approved.

    If a request is still not open 2 days after you sent it, you get a notification. Click it to send a short follow-up email. Each request is reminded once at most.

    Adds a Request box right on the block page, with Whole site / Just this page, your reasons and details, and Send request.

    When the block page already has its own request button, the box stays hidden. Turn this off to always show it.

    Updates

    Version

    New versions are checked for automatically every 2 hours, when Chrome starts and when you open the extension.

    Unzip the downloaded file and copy everything from its site-request folder into your site-request folder (replace the files). Then click Reload after update. Your settings stay.
